Where our list comes from

Built from open, trusted research.

We combine independent cosmetic ingredient lists, official textile names, and published research to make our results easier to understand.

Microplastic

On the Beat the Microbead Red List (a solid, persistent microplastic), or a synthetic fabric fiber known to shed microplastics in the wash.

Watch

On the Beat the Microbead Orange List: a synthetic polymer where it's still unclear whether it breaks down in nature.

Primary sources

Beat the Microbead Red List

Known microplastic ingredients in cosmetics and personal care (Plastic Soup Foundation; based on UNEP, Tauw and ECHA research).
Beat the Microbead Orange List

Debated synthetic polymers, which we label as Watch.
US FTC generic names for manufactured fibers

Official names used to identify synthetic textile fibers (16 CFR 303.7).
Napper & Thompson (2016) washing-machine study

Research measuring fiber release from clothes during washing (Marine Pollution Bulletin).

There is no official complete list of microplastic ingredients. We check for the most common ones, not all of the 1,000+ on the full lists.
