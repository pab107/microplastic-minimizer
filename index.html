    Where our list comes from

    Microplastic (red) = on the Beat the Microbead Red List, or a synthetic fabric fiber that sheds in the wash. Watch (amber) = on the Orange List: synthetic polymers where it's still unclear whether they break down in nature.

    • Beat the Microbead – Red List (Plastic Soup Foundation; based on UNEP, Tauw and ECHA research)
    • Beat the Microbead – Orange List
    • US FTC – generic names for manufactured fibers (16 CFR 303.7)
    • Napper & Thompson (2016), "Release of synthetic microplastic plastic fibres from domestic washing machines", Marine Pollution Bulletin

    There is no official complete list of microplastic ingredients. We check for the most common ones, not all of the 1,000+ on the full lists.

    Educational project. The watch-list is not exhaustive — always double-check labels.
